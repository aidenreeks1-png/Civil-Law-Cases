Law Learning Hub

Interactive case simulations and assessment revision activities designed to practise legal terminology, evidence analysis, jury knowledge and legal reasoning.

Class instructions: Open the activity assigned by your teacher. Read carefully, discuss or work independently as directed, and use the Back/Review controls whenever you need to revisit earlier material.
Case 01 • Core Activity

Patel v Elevate Adventure Centre

A fictional negligence case involving an accident at an indoor adventure centre.
  • Balance of probabilities
  • Duty of care and negligence
  • Weigh competing evidence
  • Contributory negligence
  • Civil jury decision-making
Recommended: whole class / groups of 4

Case 02 • Extension

Wilson v Bauer Media

A more challenging educational reconstruction based on the real Australian defamation case.
  • Defamation and civil liability
  • Evaluate competing claims and evidence
  • Distinguish liability from damages
  • Consider causation and appeal
  • Higher-level legal reasoning
Recommended: early finishers / extension

Five self-contained missions covering jury essentials, strengths and limitations, the 2006 majority-verdict reform, a Titus Day case study and extended-response argument building.
  • Multiple choice, matching and short-answer checks
  • Hints and feedback after incorrect answers
  • Go back and review earlier steps at any time
  • Assessment-focused case study and argument construction

How to use the activities

  1. Open the assigned activity above.
  2. Read the information provided before answering.
  3. Use hints only when you are genuinely stuck.
  4. Read feedback carefully and retry incorrect answers.
  5. Use Back/Review whenever you need to revisit an earlier step.
  6. For assessment revision, focus on explaining why evidence supports an argument rather than only memorising facts.

Educational resource. The Patel case is fictional. Wilson v Bauer is a simplified educational reconstruction. The NSW Jury Revision Challenge summarises legislation and case material for classroom revision.
