Year 9 Commerce • Civil Law

Civil Law Case Files

Interactive courtroom simulations designed to practise civil-law terminology, evidence analysis and legal reasoning. Work through the evidence as a jury, then justify a final decision.

Class instructions: Work with your assigned group. Discuss every piece of evidence before locking in an answer. You may use the Back/Review controls to reread earlier evidence. Record your final decision code when you finish.
Case 01 • Core Activity

Patel v Elevate Adventure Centre

A fictional negligence case involving an accident at an indoor adventure centre.
  • Balance of probabilities
  • Duty of care and negligence
  • Weigh competing evidence
  • Contributory negligence
  • Civil jury decision-making
Recommended: whole class / groups of 4

Case 02 • Extension

Wilson v Bauer Media

A more challenging educational reconstruction based on the real Australian defamation case.
  • Defamation and civil liability
  • Evaluate competing claims and evidence
  • Distinguish liability from damages
  • Consider causation and appeal
  • Higher-level legal reasoning

How to use the simulations

  1. Open the assigned case above.
  2. Read the case brief and legal reminder before starting.
  3. Analyse each item of evidence and discuss it before submitting.
  4. Use the review/back controls whenever you need to revisit earlier material.
  5. Reach a final decision using the correct civil-law standard and terminology.
  6. Record the final decision code for your teacher.

Educational resource. The Patel case is fictional. The Wilson v Bauer activity is a simplified educational reconstruction and is not a complete reproduction of the court record.
